Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about deployment, updates, and templates.

?
Can I share the dashboard with non-Odoo users?

Yes! By clicking the Share button, you can copy a unique link or generate a mobile-scannable QR Code. This loads a fully responsive, read-only layout of your dashboard without exposing Odoo backend assets.

?
Is the dashboard statistics updated in real-time?

Absolutely. All KPI indicators, charts, funnel maps, and list views pull directly from the active Odoo database objects instantly on every date or filter change.

?
Does the PDF report export respect active filters?

Yes, the backend Python PDF generator processes report actions on the server-side, carrying forward your selected filters to output a beautifully tailored document matching your screen data.

?
Does it support custom Odoo currencies?

Yes, it automatically detects the company's active currency configuration (position, formatting precision, and symbol) to format all revenues and expected pipeline counts accordingly.
